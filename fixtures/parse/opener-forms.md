Short form:

> [?a] Is this the short form?

Multi-line opener without a marker:

> [?b] First line of the opener
> continues here.
>
> Second paragraph of the opener.

Header text on later lines only:

> [?c]
> The opener starts on the second line.

Long form with an explicit marker:

> [?d]
> --> Alice Smith @ 2026-10-06T20:03-04:00
> The long form carries a name and a time.

Header text and a marker:

> [?e] Opener on the header.
>
> --> Alice
> A follow-up before any reply.
